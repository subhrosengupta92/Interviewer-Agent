Why we built it

I used to take more than 30 first rounds a month. That was 30 to 40 hours of my time, every month.

Across the team, first rounds added up to over 600 hours.

So we handed them to Talent Acquisition. But TA teams are generalists, and a generalist can't run a first round that tests the subject itself: the engineering, the deal work, the modelling.

FirstRound can. Every candidate is interviewed on the substance of the role, so the team gets its hours back and the bar stays where the experts set it.
