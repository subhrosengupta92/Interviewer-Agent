What this dashboard does

Every candidate here has done a first-round voice interview. This is where you compare them and question what they said.

  1. Pick interviews. Tick up to eight on the left, from one role or several.
  2. Compare scores. A table shows each person's first-round score by area.
  3. Read a transcript. Click a name to open the full interview on the right.
  4. Ask anything. Answers come from the transcripts, with the candidate's own words quoted.

Two sample roles are loaded so you can try it: SDE-II, Backend and Principal, Corporate Development. Interviews from your own job descriptions appear here as candidates finish them.
